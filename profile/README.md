## `> mission`

<table>
<tr>
<td width="55%" valign="middle">

> ODINT conducts **OSINT-only** security audits of government digital infrastructure for the purpose of
> **transparency, accountability and public record.**

### We document. We archive. We hold power accountable.

Collection is passive. If a system requires breaking in, it is outside the mission.

Investigations are the editorial record. The Atlas is the record by place. Hosts is the domain register:
every captured site, its composition, and the investigation that documented it.

## `> rules_of_collection`

- **Public data only.** Only publicly accessible endpoints are queried, so every finding can be reproduced.
- **Passive, always.** No exploitation, no intrusion, no unauthorized access.
- **Chain of custody.** Everything collected is timestamped and archived.
- **Sources protected.** Journalist, activist and source data is handled under our [protection policies](https://odint.io/journalist-protection/).
- **Independent.** No government or corporate influence.
